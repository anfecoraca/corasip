# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem. Report it privately to the maintainer, anfecora, by the
contact in the repository profile. Include the version, your system, and the steps to reproduce.

The maintainer aims to acknowledge a report within a week and to publish a fix or a clear plan before public
disclosure. Credit is given to the reporter unless they ask otherwise.

## Scope

corasip handles SIP signalling, media (RTP and SRTP), TLS certificates and local call history. Reports about any of
these are in scope. Certificate verification is on by default; turning it off is the user's decision and is warned
about in the interface.
